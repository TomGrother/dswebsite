About Design & Supply

The door
specialists.
Since 1986.

We design, manufacture, finish and install certified steel doorsets and slimline glazing — all from our own factory in Merthyr Tydfil.

Design & Supply was founded in 1986 by Terry Stares, in leased premises with rented equipment. We moved to Merthyr Tydfil in 1990 and to Pant Industrial Estate in 1997, where our steel door business began.

Today we manufacture LPCB-certified LPS 1175 security doorsets from SR1 to SR4, CE-marked fire doorsets tested to EN 1634-1 with certification up to 4 hours, and thermal, flood and acoustic doorsets. Our Slimline division, launched in 2017, fabricates Schüco, Jansen and Forster glazed doors, screens and façades.

Survey, CAD design, fabrication and powder coating all happen in-house, and our accredited fitters install nationwide. In 2026 a management buy-out put the business in the hands of Tom Grother, Damian Regis and Scott Davies.

07 · Slimline

Slimline
architectural
glazing.

Narrow steel profiles. Maximum daylight.
Certified performance.
SchücoJansenForster

Slimline brings narrow steel sightlines to doors, screens, windows and façades. We fabricate Schüco, Jansen and Forster systems at our factory in Merthyr Tydfil, with in-house CAD support, fabrication and powder coating.

Janisol glazed doorsets are certified to LPS 1175 and LPS 2081. Four fire-rated systems run from E30 to EI90, with Jansen Economy to E120, and five non-rated systems cover everything from internal partitions to full façades.
