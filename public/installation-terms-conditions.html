1. DEFINITIONS

(a) “the Seller” means DESIGN AND SUPPLY LIMITED.

(b) “the Buyer” means the person, company or organisation engaging the Seller to provide the Services.

(c) “the Goods” means doors, frames, glazing, ironmongery, accessories and other products supplied or installed under the Contract.

(d) “the Services” means the installation and associated activities expressly identified in the Seller’s accepted quotation or order confirmation.

(e) “the Site” means the agreed location at which delivery or installation is to take place.

(f) “the Contract” means the accepted quotation, order confirmation, agreed written variations and these Installation Conditions together with the Seller’s general terms and conditions.

(g) “the Programme” means the agreed installation dates, sequence, duration and access arrangements, as amended in writing.

(h) “the Conditions” means these Installation Terms and Conditions.

2. APPLICATION AND ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

(a) These Conditions apply to the Services and supplement the Seller’s General Sales, Delivery and Payment Terms and Conditions published at https://designandsupply.co.uk/terms.

(b) In the event of inconsistency relating specifically to installation, these Conditions shall take precedence, unless the parties expressly agree otherwise in writing. Any expressly negotiated contract terms shall take precedence to the extent agreed.

(c) Any alternative Buyer terms shall apply only where expressly accepted in writing by an authorised representative of the Seller.

(d) Nothing in these Conditions shall exclude or restrict rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.

3. QUOTATIONS AND SCOPE OF SERVICES

(a) Quotations are valid for 30 days unless stated otherwise and are based on the drawings, site information, programme and assumptions identified at tender stage.

(b) The price includes only those installation works, locations, attendances, access equipment, fixings and making-good obligations expressly specified in the quotation.

(c) Unless expressly included, the Services exclude builder’s work, structural alterations, design of supporting structures, electrical works, access-control connections, fire alarm interfacing, decorating, plastering, floor finishes, sealant to unrelated elements, temporary protection, testing by third parties and disposal of existing doors.

(d) Any additional requirements or discrepancies discovered during survey or installation shall be assessed as a variation before the affected work proceeds, where reasonably practicable.

4. DELIVERY AND DELIVERY ARRANGEMENTS

(a) The Buyer shall agree delivery dates, delivery address, operating hours, delivery vehicle restrictions and a nominated site contact in advance.

(b) Delivery dates and times are estimates unless expressly agreed as binding. The Buyer shall provide safe, lawful and unobstructed access for the specified delivery vehicle.

(c) The Buyer shall arrange any necessary delivery permits, traffic management, banksmen, road closures, booking slots and coordination with other trades, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

(d) Where the Buyer cannot accept delivery or access is unsuitable, reasonable redelivery, waiting, storage and associated costs may be charged.

(e) Delivery, unloading and installation are separate activities. Inclusion of installation in the quotation does not automatically mean unloading equipment or unloading labour is included.

5. UNLOADING, LIFTING EQUIPMENT AND OPERATIVES

(a) Unless the quotation expressly states otherwise, the Buyer shall provide, at its own cost, suitable mechanical unloading equipment and competent, appropriately trained and authorised operators to unload the Goods from the delivery vehicle.

(b) Required equipment may include a forklift, telehandler, crane, HIAB, pallet truck, lifting attachments or other equipment appropriate to the weight, dimensions, centre of gravity and packaging of the Goods.

(c) The Buyer shall ensure equipment is suitable, inspected, certified where required, of adequate capacity and available at the agreed delivery time, and that lifting operations are planned and supervised in accordance with applicable law.

(d) A safe unloading area, stable ground, adequate vehicle access, suitable exclusion zones and any required banksmen or slingers/signallers shall be provided by the Buyer.

(e) The Seller’s delivery driver or installers shall not be required to operate Buyer-provided plant or undertake lifting operations unless specifically authorised, competent and agreed in writing.

(f) If adequate unloading facilities or personnel are unavailable, the Seller may postpone unloading or delivery where reasonably necessary for safety. Reasonable waiting, abortive delivery, return transport or remobilisation charges arising from Buyer default may be recovered.

(g) Any exceptional hand-unloading arrangement must be agreed in advance and subject to a suitable manual-handling assessment. Heavy doors or glazing shall not be manually lifted where unsafe.

6. ON-SITE STORAGE AND INTERNAL DISTRIBUTION

(a) Unless expressly included, unloading, on-site storage, horizontal transport, vertical distribution and movement of Goods from the unloading point to each installation opening are the Buyer’s responsibility.

(b) The Buyer shall provide safe, level, dry, secure and adequately protected storage close to the working area, with sufficient space for handling and inspecting the Goods.

(c) Where the Seller has expressly included distribution within its scope, the quotation assumes clear routes, unobstructed doorways, suitable floor loadings and uninterrupted access to agreed goods/passenger lifts or hoists.

(d) Where lifts, hoists or mechanical handling equipment were included in the agreed site methodology, their unavailability or restricted use may require a revised method, additional resources and a variation to price and programme.

(e) The Buyer shall ensure Goods are protected against damage, theft, moisture and interference by other trades after delivery, without prejudice to any statutory or contractual rights relating to defective Goods.

7. SITE READINESS AND NOTICE TO PROCEED

(a) The Buyer shall give not less than 10 working days’ written notice that the Site is ready for the Services, unless a different notice period is agreed.

(b) All openings shall be accessible, correctly formed, dimensionally suitable and structurally sound, with appropriate fixing substrates and completed surrounding construction.

(c) Existing doors, frames, linings, timber, plasterboard, UPVC/metal architraves, trims and other obstructions shall be removed down to the agreed fixing substrate where specified as a prerequisite to installation.

(d) Any access-control equipment, cabling, magnetic locks, automatic operators, closers, security devices or services obstructing installation shall be safely isolated or removed by competent persons engaged by the Buyer, unless included in the Seller’s scope.

(e) Floor levels, thresholds, adjacent finishes, steelwork, apertures, weatherproofing interfaces and tolerances shall be confirmed before installation. Openings outside the agreed tolerance may require remedial works and additional charges.

(f) The Buyer shall confirm the Site is ready and identify any outstanding constraints before mobilisation. The Seller may request photographs or undertake an additional readiness inspection where appropriate.

8. SITE ACCESS AND FACILITIES

(a) The Buyer shall provide unrestricted and safe access to the relevant work areas during agreed working hours, including any necessary passes, permits, inductions, escorts and security clearances.

(b) Suitable parking, welfare facilities, toilets, lighting, power, water where required and a safe working environment shall be made available at no charge unless otherwise agreed.

(c) Access routes, staircases, corridors, lifts and loading areas shall be kept clear of stored materials, occupants and other trades to allow safe movement of large or heavy Goods.

(d) The Buyer shall identify restricted working hours, noise restrictions, occupied premises, live operational areas and special security or permit requirements before quotation acceptance.

(e) Additional time or cost arising from undisclosed restrictions, repeated escorting, interrupted access or unavailable facilities may be treated as a variation.

9. PROGRAMME, SEQUENCE AND PRODUCTIVITY

(a) The installation Programme, resources, number of visits and sequence shall be agreed before mobilisation and based on the scope and Site conditions known to the Seller.

(b) Any daily installation output shown in the quotation or Programme assumes complete readiness of openings, unimpeded distribution, available access equipment and normal installation conditions; it is not a guaranteed output where these assumptions are not met.

(c) The Buyer shall not direct the Seller’s operatives to alter the agreed installation sequence, workforce, method or productivity without approval from the Seller’s authorised representative.

(d) Instructions given directly to installers by other site personnel shall not vary the Contract unless confirmed by the Seller in writing.

(e) The Seller shall use reasonable endeavours to meet the agreed Programme and shall notify the Buyer of material constraints or delays of which it becomes aware.

10. DELAYS, ABORTIVE VISITS AND REMOBILISATION

(a) Where the Seller attends site but cannot reasonably commence or continue work due to Site unreadiness, access restrictions, unavailable equipment, missing approvals, other trades, unsafe conditions or other matters for which the Buyer is responsible, the Seller may recover reasonable and substantiated resulting costs.

(b) Such costs may include labour standing time, travel, vehicle costs, accommodation, plant hire, subcontractor charges, storage, additional distribution, revised lifting arrangements and remobilisation.

(c) Any additional visit shall be subject to availability and a revised Programme. The Seller is not obliged to hold resources indefinitely following a Buyer-caused postponement.

(d) Where practicable, the Seller shall notify the Buyer of the constraint and provide an opportunity to remedy it before leaving Site, except where an immediate safety concern requires cessation of work.

(e) The Seller shall take reasonable steps to mitigate avoidable additional costs.

11. HEALTH, SAFETY AND SITE MANAGEMENT

(a) The Seller shall provide and implement suitable risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) for its own installation activities and shall ensure its personnel are appropriately trained and competent.

(b) The Buyer or the party with control of the Site shall disclose relevant hazards, asbestos information, hidden services, emergency procedures, site-specific rules and the identity of the principal contractor where applicable.

(c) The Buyer shall provide a safe workplace and coordinate activities with other contractors, including exclusion zones, pedestrian segregation, traffic management and suitable welfare facilities.

(d) Where applicable, the parties shall fulfil their respective duties under the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015, the Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations 1998, the Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations 1998 and other relevant legislation.

(e) The Seller may suspend any activity it reasonably considers unsafe, pending corrective measures. Associated programme and cost implications shall be assessed according to responsibility for the unsafe condition.

12. ACCESS EQUIPMENT, WORKING AT HEIGHT AND SPECIALIST LIFTS

(a) Unless expressly included in the quotation, scaffolding, mobile elevated work platforms, towers, craneage, hoists, powered lifting aids, specialist glass-handling equipment and associated operators shall be provided by the Buyer.

(b) Equipment provided by the Buyer shall be suitable for the intended operation, inspected, erected and operated by competent persons, and available for the agreed duration.

(c) Specialist lifting plans, structural lifting assessments, permits and exclusion arrangements shall be agreed before heavy or unusual installations.

(d) If site conditions require different equipment from that reasonably anticipated, the parties shall agree any necessary variation before proceeding, save for measures required to address an immediate safety risk.

13. STRUCTURAL OPENINGS AND HIDDEN CONDITIONS

(a) The Buyer is responsible for the design, adequacy and integrity of the supporting structure, substrates, steelwork, masonry, lintels and adjacent construction, except to the extent expressly included in the Seller’s design scope.

(b) The Seller may stop and report work where hidden services, defective substrates, significant dimensional discrepancies or other unforeseen conditions prevent safe or compliant installation.

(c) Additional remedial works, specialist fixings or structural modifications shall not be undertaken unless agreed in writing and, where necessary, supported by a suitably qualified designer.

(d) Any site survey conducted by the Seller is limited to accessible and visible conditions and does not constitute a structural investigation unless expressly stated.

14. FIRE-RATED, SECURITY-RATED AND OTHER CERTIFIED PRODUCTS

(a) Installation of performance-rated products shall follow the applicable manufacturer’s instructions, assessed installation details, test/assessment evidence and specified supporting construction.

(b) The Buyer shall ensure the surrounding construction, required fire-stopping interfaces, apertures and permitted tolerances meet the applicable performance evidence and agreed design.

(c) No unauthorised cutting, drilling, alteration, hardware substitution, glazing change or connection of additional devices shall be made to certified products.

(d) Interfaces with access control, electromagnetic locks, automatic operators, alarms and other systems must be reviewed for compatibility with the product’s certification and relevant performance requirements before installation or commissioning.

(e) Any alteration or remedial proposal affecting certification shall require written technical approval before implementation. The Seller shall not be obliged to undertake an installation that would knowingly conflict with applicable evidence or law.

15. ELECTRICAL WORKS, ACCESS CONTROL AND COMMISSIONING

(a) Unless specifically included, electrical supplies, containment, cabling, controls, access-control wiring, power supplies, fire alarm interfaces and final commissioning are excluded.

(b) The Buyer shall arrange for its nominated competent contractor to complete and test such works and to be available at agreed interface points.

(c) Where commissioning is included, the Buyer shall provide live and safe power supplies, relevant system information, authorised access and the attendance of other specialist contractors as necessary.

(d) Delays or repeat attendances caused by incomplete interfacing works may be subject to reasonable additional charges.

16. WASTE, EXISTING DOORS AND HOUSEKEEPING

(a) Unless expressly included, removal and disposal of existing doors, frames, architectural materials and building waste from other trades are excluded.

(b) The Seller shall collect and place waste generated directly by its installation activities in a designated on-site collection location, provided a suitable location is available and agreed.

(c) Removal of that waste from Site, skips and disposal fees shall be the Buyer’s responsibility unless the quotation expressly includes off-site disposal.

(d) The Buyer shall identify any hazardous waste and arrange its management by appropriately authorised specialists.

(e) On completion of an attendance, the Seller shall leave its immediate working area reasonably tidy, subject to Site arrangements and safe access.

17. VARIATIONS AND ADDITIONAL WORKS

(a) Any change to quantity, specification, handing, ironmongery, opening preparation, installation method, floor distribution, access arrangements, Programme or sequence may constitute a variation.

(b) Variations must be requested or confirmed in writing by authorised representatives. The Seller shall identify price and programme implications where reasonably practicable before commencing varied work.

(c) Where urgent safety or protective works are reasonably required, the Seller may undertake the minimum necessary actions and notify the Buyer promptly.

(d) No employee, operative or subcontractor on Site has authority to agree a commercial variation unless expressly authorised by the Seller.

18. INSPECTION, PRACTICAL COMPLETION AND SIGN-OFF

(a) The Buyer shall make an authorised representative available to inspect completed installation works and sign a completion or handover record at the agreed time.

(b) If no representative attends, the Seller may issue a dated completion report supported by photographs and a list of any outstanding items. Absence of a signature shall not, by itself, mean the Services were not performed.

(c) The Buyer shall notify the Seller promptly in writing of visible defects or incomplete items, giving reasonable detail and allowing an opportunity for inspection and rectification.

(d) Minor snagging not materially preventing intended use may be recorded separately and completed within a reasonable agreed period, subject to access and materials.

(e) Final completion, acceptance and any statutory rights or obligations shall be determined by the Contract and applicable law; a site signature is evidence of attendance and condition, not a waiver of latent defects.

19. PROTECTION OF COMPLETED WORK AND DAMAGE

(a) Unless included in the quotation, temporary protective coverings, boarding, guarding and subsequent protection of installed Goods are the Buyer’s responsibility following installation.

(b) The Buyer shall prevent interference, misuse or damage by other trades, site users and follow-on works.

(c) The Seller shall remain responsible for damage caused by its negligence or breach of Contract. Damage due to other parties or post-installation interference shall be assessed separately and any requested remedial work may be chargeable.

20. WARRANTIES, DEFECTS AND AFTERCARE

(a) The Seller’s applicable product and workmanship warranties shall operate in accordance with the Contract and the Seller’s published General Terms, subject to non-excludable statutory rights.

(b) The Buyer shall follow manufacturer maintenance, operating and inspection instructions, including for door closers, hinges, locks, seals, threshold components and powered equipment.

(c) The Buyer shall notify alleged defects in writing with location, description and supporting photographs where reasonably available, and permit reasonable access for inspection.

(d) Where an attendance reveals no defect attributable to the Seller or a fault outside warranty, reasonable call-out, travel, labour and materials charges may apply if notified and supported.

(e) No provision shall exclude liability for defective installation or Goods where exclusion is prohibited by law.

21. PRICE, PAYMENT AND SUSPENSION

(a) Installation charges, stage payments, invoice due dates and any agreed retention shall be as stated in the Contract, read with the Seller’s General Terms to the extent lawfully applicable.

(b) Reasonable and evidenced variations, delay costs and additional attendances may be invoiced in accordance with the Contract.

(c) The Seller may suspend Services for material non-payment where permitted by the Contract and applicable law, following any notice required by law, including the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 where applicable.

(d) Nothing in these Conditions shall remove statutory payment, notice, adjudication or suspension rights applying to a construction contract.
